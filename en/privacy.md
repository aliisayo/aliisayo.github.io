## Data processed

**Visit statistics (GoatCounter).**
To learn which pages and links interest visitors, this website uses
[GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), an analytics tool **without cookies or persistent
identifiers**. It records the page viewed, the sections displayed, clicks on contact links, the
referring site, browser and operating system type, screen size and approximate country.
According to GoatCounter's documentation, the IP address is only used to avoid counting the same
visit twice and is not stored. Purpose: improving the website's content (legitimate interest).

**Hosting (GitHub Pages).**
Like any host, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses in its technical logs for the security and
operation of the service. See
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**E-mail exchanges.**
If you write to me, I use your address and message only to reply. They are kept for the duration
of the exchange, then deleted on request.

**Language preference.**
Your FR / EN choice is stored in your browser (local storage). It is never sent anywhere; you can
erase it by clearing the website's data.

**Typefaces.**
They are hosted with the website: no request is sent to Google or any other third party.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by
writing to [{{ id.email }}](mailto:{{ id.email }}).

You can also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority: in Niger, the
**Haute Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel (HAPDP)**; if you live in the
European Union, the authority of your country.

## Legal framework

Niger Law No. 2017-28 of 3 May 2017 on the protection of personal data and, for visitors residing
in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
